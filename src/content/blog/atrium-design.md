## Bureaucracy meets science fiction

The G.R.A.C.E. atrium takes an ordinary experience—waiting in line—and reimagines it as a strange futuristic facility. Number dispensers, waiting chairs, queue barriers, and large displays establish the space's purpose before a single line of dialogue.

## A place with a history

The corridors branching from the atrium connect to different systems and challenges. Each area should feel like it belongs to the facility while revealing something distinct about how this world operates.

## The next pass

Blockout establishes the layout. Lighting, sound, signage, and smaller environmental details will do much of the storytelling work from here.
