## More than a menu

G.R.A.C.E. started as an interactive terminal system, but quickly became an opportunity to tell stories through the environment. A terminal can provide useful information and still have personality.

The goal is to make interacting with a machine feel like discovering a piece of the world.

## Building the foundation

The system uses Unreal Engine Blueprints, with a reusable parent terminal and child Blueprints for variations. That structure makes it easier to give each terminal its own behavior without duplicating every piece of logic.

## What comes next

The next step is to connect more terminals throughout the facility and give each one a reason to exist, from diagnostics to personal journal entries.
